Our Services

BzPayroll provides a complete payroll service designed to meet the needs of modern businesses. From calculating wages to filing taxes, every step of the payroll process is streamlined and automated.

Core Payroll Processing

Our core services include employee payroll processing, tax calculation, and compliance management. BzPayroll automatically calculates gross pay, deductions, benefits, and net pay based on your company's rules and local regulations. Federal, state, and local payroll taxes are computed accurately, ensuring every pay cycle is compliant.

Multiple Pay Types & Employee Services

We support multiple pay types, including hourly, salaried, commission-based, and contract workers. Direct deposit allows employees to receive their pay quickly and securely. Digital pay stubs and employee self-service portals reduce administrative workload and improve transparency.

Tax Reporting & Year-End Forms

BzPayroll also handles tax reporting and year-end forms such as W-2s and 1099s. Businesses can generate and file these forms electronically, eliminating the stress of manual preparation. For multi-state employers, BzPayroll manages varying tax rates and compliance rules automatically.

Integrated with BzComposer, our payroll services synchronize directly with accounting records. Payroll expenses, liabilities, and tax payments flow seamlessly into your financial system, ensuring accurate books without duplicate data entry. Accountants and payroll professionals can oversee multiple clients within one unified environment.

In addition, BzPayroll provides reporting tools for payroll summaries, tax liabilities, labor costs, and compliance audits. These insights help business owners make informed decisions and plan confidently.

With BzPayroll, payroll becomes a predictable, automated process—freeing you from paperwork, calculations, and compliance concerns.
