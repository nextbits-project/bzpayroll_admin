What Is BzPayroll?

BzPayroll is a dedicated payroll management platform designed to handle one of the most sensitive and critical business functions: paying people correctly and on time. Built as a specialized solution within the BzComposer ecosystem, BzPayroll focuses entirely on payroll accuracy, compliance, and efficiency.

Automated Payroll Lifecycle

At its foundation, BzPayroll automates the entire payroll lifecycle. From collecting time and salary data to calculating wages, deductions, and taxes, every step is processed with precision. The system ensures employees are paid correctly, whether they are hourly, salaried, commissioned, or contract-based.

Tax Compliance & Accuracy

BzPayroll continuously applies federal, state, and local tax rules, keeping businesses compliant without manual research or guesswork. Payroll taxes are calculated automatically, and required reports and filings are generated with accuracy. This reduces risk, prevents costly errors, and ensures regulatory requirements are always met.

Employee & Employer Benefits

Employees benefit from direct deposit, digital pay stubs, and self-service access to their payroll records. Employers gain control, visibility, and peace of mind through clear dashboards and detailed payroll reports.

What makes BzPayroll unique is its seamless integration with BzComposer's accounting and business management system. Payroll data flows directly into financial records—posting expenses, liabilities, and payments in real time. This eliminates duplicate entry and keeps books perfectly aligned with payroll activity.

BzPayroll is built for businesses that value reliability. It transforms payroll from a recurring headache into a smooth, automated process. Whether you run a small team or a growing organization, BzPayroll gives you the confidence that your people are paid correctly, your records are accurate, and your business remains compliant—every pay period.
